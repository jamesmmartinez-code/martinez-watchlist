In Whisperwood, where moonlight drips like silver, the trees don luminous leaves for one magical night. This spectacle inspired the Festival of Luminous Leaves, a celebration of wonder and friendship. Players can participate by crafting lanterns, sharing stories, and dancing under the radiant canopy.
