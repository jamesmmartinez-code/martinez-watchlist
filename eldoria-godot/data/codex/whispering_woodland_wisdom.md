In the Whispering Woods, the ancient trees whisper secrets to those who listen. The wind carries the whispers, sharing tales of old magic and forgotten lore. The wise ones say that if you listen closely, you can hear the trees warning of dangers to come, and guiding you towards hidden wonders. Will you listen to the whispers of the woods?
