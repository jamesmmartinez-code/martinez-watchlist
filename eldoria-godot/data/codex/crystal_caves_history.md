Deep within the Crystal Caves, an ancient song echoes through the shimmering tunnels. It is said that the crystals themselves sang in harmony with the land, guiding the first settlers to hidden wonders and secret paths. As you explore the caves, listen for the whispers of the past, and perhaps you'll uncover the melody that shaped the world.
