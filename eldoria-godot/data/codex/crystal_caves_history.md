Deep within the Crystal Caves, ancient melodies echo through glittering caverns. It's said the crystals themselves sang to the earliest dwellers, teaching them the secrets of the earth and the harmony of the land. The whispers of these songs still linger, guiding adventurers through the shimmering tunnels.
