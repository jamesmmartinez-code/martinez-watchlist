Deep within the Crystal Caves, ancient songs echo through glittering caverns. The caves were formed when the land was young and music was a powerful magic. Over time, the melodies infused the crystals, creating a harmony that still resonates today. As you wander the caves, listen for the whispers of the past, and you might just hear the faintest hint of a long-forgotten tune.
