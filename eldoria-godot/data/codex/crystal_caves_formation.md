Deep beneath Eldoria's roots, crystalline structures shimmer with an ethereal glow. Formed over millennia, these glittering caverns whisper secrets of the land's ancient heartbeat. As adventurers delve into the Crystal Caves, they unravel mysteries hidden since the Sundering, and forge unbreakable bonds with the land itself.
