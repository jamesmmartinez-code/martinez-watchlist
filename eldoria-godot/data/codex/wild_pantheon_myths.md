In the Whisperwood's heart, where sunbeams filter through emerald leaves, the Wild Pantheon's myths unfold. It's said the ancient trees whispered secrets to the forest's creatures, birthing tales of the Moon Fox, the River Dragon, and the Leaf King. These stories wove a tapestry of wonder, as the forest's inhabitants learned to live in harmony with nature. The Wild Pantheon's myths remind us that even the smallest creatures can shape the world's destiny.
