title: "The Celestial Gathering"
---

When starlight pours upon the meadow's heart, where moonflowers bloom and ancient trees take part, the guardians of wonder will convene, their bond of friendship forged like a celestial dream. In harmony, they'll dance beneath the radiant beam, as the forest awakens with a gentle, ethereal theme. The melody of unity will echo, wild and free, a symphony that beckons thee. Seek the harmony that only friendship can bring, and the celestial gathering will be the key to unlock and sing.
