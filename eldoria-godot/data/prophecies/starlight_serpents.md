id: starlight_serpents
kind: prophecy
title: "Moonlit Slither"
---

When moonbeams dance upon the lake,
A serpent's whisper will the winds partake.
Three ancient songs will echo through the night,
As starlight serpents weave their gentle light.
In shimmering scales, a hidden path's revealed,
To secrets kept, where myths and magic yield.
The heart of wonder will be made to see,
The magic that's been waiting there for thee.
