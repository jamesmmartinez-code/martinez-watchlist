## Two valid DPS models — note for downstream agents

`difficulty_targets.md` (PX-owned) uses **kid-effective DPS** = 0.50 × theoretical max (movement, whiffs, distraction). That's the right ceiling for "what does the kid actually achieve." Bands are **2.0–4.0 s** (trash) … **40–70 s** (boss).

`data/creatures/_dmg_curve.gd::EFFECTIVE_DPS` (bestiary-owned) uses **conservative averaged DPS** with crit and weapon curve baked in. That's the floor for "what's the band-appropriate player capable of without trying." Bands documented in `_dmg_curve.gd`: trash 5–9 s, elite 18–30 s, boss 90–180 s.

The numbers DON'T align because they measure different things. **Both should be kept** — bestiary should land in BOTH bands when re-tuning. Action item: a future PX run should reconcile the two band tables in `difficulty_targets.md` so the bestiary has one unambiguous target sheet to read against. Filed.
