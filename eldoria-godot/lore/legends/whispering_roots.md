title: "The Whispering Roots of Whisperwood"
region: "whisperwood"
---

In Whisperwood, where ancient trees whisper secrets to the wind, a young hero named Eira discovered she could hear their whispers. With the trees' guidance, Eira saved the forest from a terrible curse, and the trees still whisper their thanks to those who listen. The people of Whisperwood say that on quiet nights, you can still hear Eira's name being whispered by the trees, reminding all of the bravery and kindness that saved their home.
