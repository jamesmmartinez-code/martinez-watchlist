title: "The Luminous Guide"
region: "skyreave"
---

Long ago, when the skies were darkest, a young girl named Aria ventured out into the unknown, searching for a way to bring light to her people. She climbed the tallest mountain and reached for the stars, and as she did, a brilliant light engulfed her. From that day on, Aria became the North Star, guiding travelers through the treacherous skies of Skyreave, and her spirit remains, shining bright, to lead those who seek adventure and wisdom.
