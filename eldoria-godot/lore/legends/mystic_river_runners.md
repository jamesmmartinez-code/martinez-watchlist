Long ago, when the moon was full and the river was swollen, the River Runners would ride the currents of Whisperwood on great wooden rafts. Their laughter and shouts echoed through the forest as they chased the fish and the fun, their hearts full of joy and wonder. The River Runners were a group of brave and daring young adventurers who explored every bend and twist of the river, discovering hidden waterfalls and secret caves. They were said to have possessed a deep understanding of the river and its rhythms, and were often called upon by the villagers to help with the annual fish harvest. To this day, the people of Whisperwood still tell stories of the River Runners and their wild adventures, and the river remains a place of magic and mystery, where the sound of laughter and the thrill of discovery still linger in the air.
