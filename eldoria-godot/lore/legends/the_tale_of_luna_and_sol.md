title: "The Tale of Luna and Sol"
region: "whisperwood"
---

Long ago, in the heart of Whisperwood, the twin gods Luna and Sol danced under the starry sky. As they twirled, their footsteps created the gentle streams and their laughter brought forth the soft rustle of leaves. The people of Whisperwood believe that on quiet nights, you can still hear the twins' joyful whispers carried on the wind, reminding all of the magic that lies just beyond the treeline.
