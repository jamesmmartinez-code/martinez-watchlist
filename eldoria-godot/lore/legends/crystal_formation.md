In the depths of the Crystal Caves, a mystical formation of crystals hums with an otherworldly energy. Locals claim that on quiet nights, you can hear the crystals whispering ancient secrets to those who listen closely. Many have attempted to decipher the whispers, but none have succeeded. The crystals remain a source of wonder and awe, a reminder of the magic that lies just beyond our understanding.
