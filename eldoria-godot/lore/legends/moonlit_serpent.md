title: "The Moonlit Serpent of Whisperwood"
region: "whisperwood"
---

In Whisperwood, where moonbeams dance through silver leaves, a legend tells of a serpent with scales that shimmer like stardust. This mystical creature, born from the dreams of the forest, roams the shadows, guiding lost travelers home under the gentle light of the full moon. With a whisper, it can calm the fiercest of beasts and with a glance, it can heal the deepest of wounds. The people of Whisperwood believe that on nights when the moon is full, if you listen closely, you can hear the soft rustle of its scales, a reminder of the magic that lies just beyond the edge of everyday sight.
