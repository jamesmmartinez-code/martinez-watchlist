title: "Moonlit Melody"
---

I remember the night the faeries sang beneath the whispering leaves. Their lullaby, a gentle hush of moonbeams and forest dreams, still echoes within me. I've tried to recall the melody, but it vanishes like mist at dawn. If you find this, follow the moonlit stream to the old oak; perhaps the song remains, hidden in the silence.
