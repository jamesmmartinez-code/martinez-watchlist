title: "Mystery of the Missing Press"
---

I can hardly recall the last time our village pressed its own fruit. The old press, passed down from Grandma Lily, has gone missing. Some say the traveling performers took it, while others claim the forest fey borrowed it for their moonlight festivities. If you find it, please return it to us. The children are eager for fresh cider this autumn.
