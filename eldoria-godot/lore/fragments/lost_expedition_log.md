I've been tracking the feral wisps for weeks, but they vanish like mist at dawn. The ancient trees seem to whisper secrets to each other, and I'm starting to suspect they're hiding something. The wind carries a faint melody, a siren's call that beckons me deeper. I fear I'm not the only one searching for the Heartwood's source.
