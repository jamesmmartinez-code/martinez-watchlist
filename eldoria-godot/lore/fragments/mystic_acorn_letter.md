title: "A Whispered Warning"
---

I've seen them, the Shadow Creatures, lurking at the forest's edge. They covet the ancient trees, seeking to snuff their gentle glow. I dare not speak of this aloud, but I've left a token of warning: a small, glowing acorn, hidden where the moonlight filters through. Seek it, dear one, and beware the darkness that stirs.
