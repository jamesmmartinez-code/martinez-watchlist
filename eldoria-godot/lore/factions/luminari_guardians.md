The Luminari Guardians are a group of skilled warriors and scholars dedicated to protecting the Whisperwood and uncovering its secrets. They believe in living in harmony with nature and preserving the ancient knowledge that lies within the forest. Led by Eira Shadowglow, the Guardians are fiercely dedicated to their mission and will stop at nothing to defend their home and the creatures that live within it. They are known for their bravery, wisdom, and unwavering commitment to justice.
