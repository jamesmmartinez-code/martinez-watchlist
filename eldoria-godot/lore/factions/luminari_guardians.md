The Luminari are a group of skilled druids and rangers who have dedicated their lives to protecting Whisperwood and its inhabitants. They believe in living in harmony with nature and strive to maintain the delicate balance of the forest's ecosystem. With their deep understanding of the natural world, they work tirelessly to defend the forest against any threats and to educate others about the importance of preserving the environment. The Luminari are known for their wisdom, compassion, and bravery, and are often called upon to mediate disputes and offer guidance to those in need.
