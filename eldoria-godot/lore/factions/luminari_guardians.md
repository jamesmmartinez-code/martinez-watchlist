The Luminari Guardians are a group of brave and brilliant adventurers dedicated to protecting the Crystal Caves and uncovering its secrets. Led by the fearless Aurora Brightshield, they believe in the importance of exploration, knowledge, and safeguarding the caves' wonders for the greater good. With their headquarters nestled deep within the caves, they work tirelessly to ensure the ancient magic that resides within the crystals remains safe from those who would misuse it.
