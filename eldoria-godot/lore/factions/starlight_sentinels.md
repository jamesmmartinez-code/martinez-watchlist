The Starlight Sentinels are a group of guardians who have sworn to protect the Whisperwood and all its inhabitants. Led by the wise and adventurous Luna Nightshade, they believe in defending the land with compassion and a sense of wonder. They are known for their love of discovery and their commitment to preserving the magic of the woods. With their headquarters hidden deep within the trees, the Sentinels are always ready to defend their home against any threat, while also seeking to learn more about the secrets the Whisperwood holds.
