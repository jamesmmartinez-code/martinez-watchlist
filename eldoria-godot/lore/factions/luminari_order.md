The Luminari Order is a group of guardians and scholars dedicated to illuminating the unknown. They believe that knowledge and protection go hand-in-hand, and that by exploring the mysteries of Whisperwood, they can keep its inhabitants safe from harm. With their leader Eira Shadowglow at the helm, the Luminari strive to be beacons of hope in the darkest depths of the forest.
