The Luminari Order is a mystical group dedicated to preserving the delicate balance of nature within Whisperwood. They believe that every living creature plays a vital role in the forest's ecosystem and strive to maintain harmony among its inhabitants. With their deep understanding of the forest's ancient magic, they work tirelessly to protect it from those who would seek to exploit its power. The Order is led by the wise and enigmatic Eira Shadowglow, who possesses a unique connection to the forest and its secrets.
