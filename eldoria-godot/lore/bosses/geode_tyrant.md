# Geodelich, the Sundered Heart — A Boss Reading

*Boss-lore canon for the Crystal Caves' resonant under-heart. Sister-document
to `lore/bosses/goblin_warlord.md` (the Whisperwood-fold's standing wound,
written first; this is the second boss-reading on the shelf), and to the
codex fragments that already hold the cave's silence — `data/codex/pale_wyrm_beneath.md`
(the Wyrm's* mhirren *under the stone), `data/codex/stag_courts_courtesy.md`
(the under-stream's cold-iron air; the Antler Crown's* ai-mhorren), and
`data/codex/vellums_spine.md` (the* vellath, *the* thol-ennen, *the
* caer-vellis *of mason-canon). This document does not invent the
mechanical kit — that belongs to the Bestiary Designer in
`data/bosses/geode_tyrant.kit.tres`, where Geodelich is set down at level
eight, tier three, *act-2 capstone*. It writes the **voice** of the
encounter so that every other agent (kit, environment, audio, dialogue,
item, codex) has a load-bearing story to defer to.*

This document is **child-safe** by THEME §7. The brothers will still raise
a sword, and Geodelich will still strike at them with crystal and chime.
Combat does not break. What this document does is hold the cave's verb
steady: Geodelich is **laid down,** not slain; the chime is **closed,**
not silenced; the heart, in the lore-side reading, *finishes its long
remembering* and is at last given the rest a thing earns when it has been
allowed, after a long strangeness, to be **set down where it was meant to
be.** The dignity is in the unhurried sadness. Future agents who turn
Geodelich triumphal break canon. Geodelich is older than the village; the
village is younger than the wound; the closing is owed.

## What Geodelich is, in this world

Geodelich is **not a necromancer.** The kit-comments name a *crystallized
necromancer* and a *resonant heart of the under-stream* in the same line,
and the kit-comments are the kit-comments. The lore reading is the
**second half** of that compound, slowly told.

A long while before the lanterns of Briarwood — long enough that the
mason-line that wrote `data/codex/vellums_spine.md` was not yet a
mason-line, only a hand-cousin of the Stag-Court's outer scribes — there
was a **ward-stone** set into the spine of the world. It was *vellath* in
the strict mason-sense: a **keeping-stone,** the first stone of a course,
the stone the wall above it would never have to be tested twice for. It
was set true. It was tapped twice. It was *thol-ennen*-spoken under the
breath, in the older word a mason says when a stone has gone in true and
the mortar will not be needed twice. It was **meant to remain a vellath
forever,** because forever was, in the long sense the older powers used
the word, a span any *vellath* could carry without fraying.

Then the Sundering came, and Vellum the Patient Stone broke against the
Pale Wyrm, and the world cracked between them, and the cave's outer wall
cracked with it, and the *vellath* — the keeping-stone of an older
ward-line whose other stones the canon does not name — was **lifted out
of true** by the cracking. It was not destroyed. It was *displaced.* The
mason-canon would say it was *un-set;* the Stag-Court reading would say
it was, more carefully, **un-laid-down.** The rest of the older
ward-line's stones were, by canon held in `vellums_spine.md`, taken back
into the *caer-vellis* — the spine that holds — and re-set somewhere
deeper than the Crystal Caves' present floor. The keeping-stone was
*not.* The keeping-stone was left where the cracking had pushed it: in
the resonance chamber the kit-comments name, on the lip of the
under-stream, half-seated and half-listening.

A *vellath* that has been un-laid-down does not stop being what it was
made to be. It goes on **trying to keep.** And a *vellath* that has been
trying to keep for a Sundering and the long quiet after a Sundering and
the slow centuries since — without any wall above it to keep, and
without any mason returning to *thol-ennen* it back into trueness — will,
the Stag-Court's outer scribes wrote in a leaf the canon does not yet
preserve, **begin to remember itself instead of the wall.** The
crystallization the kit-comments describe is what *that* remembering
looks like, set down in the cave's blue-glow rock. The chime is what
that remembering **sounds like** when the air is asked.

Geodelich is not a necromancer. Geodelich is a **keeping-stone that has,
in the long un-laid-down centuries, become its own course.** The other
half of the kit-comment compound — the *crystallized necromancer* — is
what the **south-country priests** call this when they read the cave's
chime through an Iron-Crown lens. They are wrong, in the same charitable
way they are wrong about the Pale Wyrm being dead (`pale_wyrm_beneath.md`).
The wrongness keeps the smoke-cities steady. We who walk the valley have
to know better, because the chime will tell us.

Future agents may not write Geodelich as a *necromancer in the
south-country sense.* No raised dead. No grave-robbing flavor. No bone.
The crystallization is **stone-flavored,** not carrion-flavored. THEME
§7 governs.

## The under-stream and the cracked wall

The encounter sits in the **resonance chamber** the kit-comments name —
the deepest readable chamber of the Crystal Caves, where the under-stream
runs along the wall on the down-slope side and the cave-air, by canon,
*smells of cold iron* (`stag_courts_courtesy.md`). The chamber's outer
wall is the wall the Sundering cracked. The crack is still there. The
canon-line in `pale_wyrm_beneath.md` — *the Crystal Caves are the wound
itself, still unhealed, where the sky leaks through into the dark* — is
not metaphor. The crack lets a thread of the deeper-than-cave dark up
into the chamber. Geodelich sits on the lip of the under-stream with the
crack at its back.

The chamber is **not** dark in the encounter. The blue glow the cave
uses elsewhere brightens here to a steady **fey-cyan** lantern-color
(THEME §3 magic palette, `#65DFE5`). The brightening is Geodelich's
*remembering* leaking sideways into the rock; the rock has been near a
keeping-stone long enough to learn the keeping-stone's habits. The
brightening is **not** an alarm. Future audio agents who score the
chamber-entry with a sting break canon. The right cue is the
chamber-glow rising slowly from the player's hand-shadow as they cross
the threshold, and the chime, faint at first, *like a kettle just off
the hob* — the canonical phrase from `pale_wyrm_beneath.md`, here
deliberately quoted to compose. The brothers will know they have come
somewhere old. The cave will not need to tell them.

The under-stream itself is **not interactable** in the encounter. It is
*kerritha*-d in its own way — water laid down so the land may hold it —
and the rule from `vellums_spine.md` applies: *if you are not of a
forge, please put this back where you found it, and walk gently.* The
brothers walk gently. The under-stream is the mason's verb in water.

## The chime, not the roar

The kit-comments line reads: *It does not roar. It chimes; the chime
gets louder as it is hurt.* This is the load-bearing voice-rule of the
encounter. Geodelich does not howl, snarl, growl, scream, hiss, or
otherwise carrion-vocalize. It **chimes.** The chime is a *kept-witness
clearing its throat* — a sound stone makes only when the air is asked
on its behalf. The chime is louder when Geodelich is hurt because **the
remembering is deeper when the keeping-stone is closer to being
re-disturbed.** A *vellath* that has been un-laid-down for centuries
remembers, in its slow stone-tongue, every laying-down ever pressed
into it; when the brothers strike it, the strikes loosen one more
laying-down each, and one more chime escapes, and the chime is the
**voice** of the laying-down going free.

The chime is **not melodic.** The audio agent's bank should hold a
*low, mid, and high resonance layer* (the kit-comments queue this), and
the layers should add to one another as the encounter goes on, but the
layers must **not** form a tune. A tune would be triumphalist. A chime
that adds to itself the way a wall is added to course-by-course is
correct. The closer reference is **the kettle just off the hob** plus
**the mason's testing-tap on a true vellath, struck with the knuckle
not the chisel** (`vellums_spine.md`). Add those two, and add a third
layer that is the **fey-cyan air itself learning the chime by being near
it,** and the audio is in canon.

When Geodelich falls, the chime **laps once more, slower, and then is
laid down.** It does not cut off. It does not echo. It is, by canon,
*kerrithen-corr* — the laying-down-that-has-completed — and the chamber
is the silence after. The audio cue at the close is **one knuckle-tap on
true stone, two breaths later, and then under-stream water.** No
triumph music. THEME §6.

## Two new Old Faerie words this artifact sets down

The lexicon stood at **thirty-four** at the close of
`lore/bosses/goblin_warlord.md` (the *mhain-thra* and *thrael-hoir*
additions). This document brings it to **thirty-six.** Both new words
are mason-cousin words — not Stag-Court reservations, not goblin-fold
reservations — because the keeping-stone half of Geodelich is the
**older mason-line's lost keeping-stone,** and the words must come
from that line.

### ***thirren-vael*** *(THEER-en-VAYL)* — "the calling-out of remembered stone"

A chime, not a song. The sound a *thirre* — a place where time pools —
makes when the air is asked on its behalf. In the strict older
mason-cousin reading, *thirren-vael* is what happens when a *vellath*
that has been un-laid-down for too long has the patience to remember a
laying-down loosened back out of it: the air is asked, and the air
**answers in the shape of the stone's keeping.** Not a tune. A
*knowing* the air does on behalf of stone.

The word is reserved for **stones that have kept and will keep again.**
A struck cobble does not *thirren-vael.* A cracked wall-stone does not
*thirren-vael.* A *vellath* that has been waiting to be re-laid-down,
or a *thirre* that has been listening at half-a-depth long enough to
have gathered chime — these *thirren-vael.* Geodelich's chime, the
herbalists' canopy-quiet after a forest-mass settles, the tone the
well-stone gives off on Longnight when the rope-lantern is lit at the
mouth — these are *thirren-vael* in escalating registers.

The word is **not** applied to a forge or a hearth. Brigid's mark on
Edda's anvil rings *otherwise;* the smith-tongue's verbs are
*haethe-cousin,* not *thirren-cousin* (cf. `data/codex/brigids_ribbon.md`,
authoring notes). Future agents who *thirren-vael* a smithing tap break
the cousin-rule. The mason-cousin and the smith-cousin are **near**
families and are not the **same** family.

### ***vellath-corr*** *(VELL-ath-kor)* — "the keeping-stone set true"

A mason's compound — *vellath* (the laid foundation, from
`vellums_spine.md`) plus the older root *-corr,* which is the
mason-cousin of the same syllable that the steppe-side speech uses for
*riding-through* (`three_crowns.md`, the Stone Crown's *korr*). Where
the steppe's *korr* is *the riding-through-and-not-stopping,* the
mason's *-corr* is *the having-set-down-and-not-needing-to-touch-again.*
A *vellath-corr* is a keeping-stone that has been set true and will
hold three generations without fraying.

Geodelich, by canon, **was** a *vellath-corr* before the Sundering. It
is one of the load-bearing voice-rules of the encounter that the
brothers, in raising a sword, are **not** acting against the
*vellath-corr* itself. They are acting against the **un-laying-down**
the Sundering pressed onto it. The keeping-stone, in the lore-side
reading, *wants* to be a *vellath-corr* again. It cannot, because no
mason of the older ward-line is left to *thol-ennen* it back. The
brothers' work is the **second-best courtesy:** a *vellath-corr* that
cannot be re-laid-down is at least allowed, in the closing, to **stop
trying to keep a wall that is no longer there.** The encounter is, in
the village's older verb, **a release of the long keeping.**

This word may not be used of any other stone in the present canon.
Edda's anvil is not a *vellath-corr* (it is a *haethe*-bearer; cousin
register). The well's keeping-stone is a *vellath* but not yet a
*vellath-corr* — three generations have not yet completed; ask again
in two Maeve-knots. The Vigil shelf's cedar plank is not a stone. The
south-plank where the Warlord's iron crown may yet be *kerritha*-d
(`whisperwood_goblins.md`) is, by mason-reading, **a candidate**
*vellath-corr,* and would become one if the village ever sets the
crown on it and leaves it for three generations untouched. That is a
hook the next faction-politics or codex run may pick up, but **only
that one** stone qualifies for future *vellath-corr* status. The word
is not loose.

The lexicon is now at **thirty-six.** The Stone-Tongue cap holds at
three (the steppe-side cap canonized in `world.md`). The Stag-Court's
reserved words (*thrael-hoir,* *ai-mhorren,* *vael-i-thirren,*
*velhain-tor*) are not added to. The goblin-fold's lost glossary
remains at three. The mason-cousin lexicon, with this run's two
additions, now stands at six (counting the existing *vellath,*
*thol-ennen,* *caer-vellis,* *kerrithen-corr,* and the two new
words; the goblin-warlord run's *mhain-thra* and *thrael-hoir* count
toward the Stag-Court column and the *general* column respectively,
not the mason column).

## The encounter, in voice

The encounter is **fought** by the player and the player's brother
(the kit's level-eight band lands at chainmail and steel-blade tier;
the brothers are reading the chamber a tier below the Whisperwood
Warlord and a tier above the goblin-brute clear). The fight is real.
Telegraphs are kid-readable per THEME and the boss-kit rules
(`data/bosses/_boss_kit.gd`). What follows is the **voice** of the
three phases the Bestiary Designer has set down at the kit level
(`p1_warden,` `p2_awakened,` and the implied third phase the
`shard_storm` tell rules into). Voice is the lorekeeper's territory;
mechanics belong to the kit.

### Phase one — the chime, even (`p1_warden`)

Geodelich sits on the lip of the under-stream. The blue glow brightens
slowly as the brothers cross. The chime begins **even** — a single
mid-resonance layer, faint, patient, the kettle just off the hob. The
keeping-stone is, in this phase, **trying to keep the chamber the way
a wall would have been kept.** Two crystal elementals form at the
flanking pillars on phase-enter (the kit specifies this); they are not
servants in the necromancer-sense. They are **chips of the same
keeping-stone** that have crystallized off the *vellath* the way a
mason's keeping-stone, set wrong, will sometimes spall a corner. They
mean nothing. The brothers may break them or step around them. Either
is canonical.

The *sundering_chime* tell — the kit's first-encounter opener at
1100 ms — is, by lore, the keeping-stone's **first** loosening. A
single laying-down, pressed into the *vellath* in the older
ward-line's first course, escapes back into the chamber-air. The
chime is louder for the laying-down's release. The fey-cyan ring
that charges in the VFX is the **shape of the laying-down** — a
keeping-circle the older mason walked once around the keeping-stone
to seal the course. The brothers do not know what they are seeing.
They will know to step out of the ring. That is enough.

The *crystal_quake* tell at 900 ms is the under-stream **answering**
the loosened laying-down. Stone-on-water makes the floor groan; the
mason-canon would say the chamber's floor is **remembering it is a
floor.** The brothers feel it through their boots. The cave is, in
this phase, *thirren-vael*-ing at low register.

### Phase two — the chime, deeper (`p2_awakened,` 70% HP)

At seventy-percent HP the keeping-stone has lost enough laying-downs
that it begins to **walk.** This is the kit's *slow_walker* tag, and
the lore-side reading is that the *vellath* is, in the Stag-Court's
outer scribes' phrase from a leaf the canon does not yet preserve,
**looking for the wall it was meant to keep.** It will not find one.
There is no wall. The wall it kept was a course of the older
ward-line, and the older ward-line was taken back into the
*caer-vellis* during the Sundering. The keeping-stone is walking a
floor that has nothing to be set under, and it is doing so in the
slow, patient cadence of a stone that has not been hurried in
centuries.

The *prismatic_echo* tell at 1200 ms is the kit's delayed-AoE
mechanic — *the floor lights first.* Lore reading: the *vellath* is
**casting back** the laying-down that was *thol-ennen*-spoken into it
when it was first set true. The fey-cyan lattice that lights the floor
is the *thol-ennen* itself, in old-mason-glyph, and the delayed strike
is the *vellath* pressing the *thol-ennen* back **outward** in the
hope that the chamber will receive it as a true-laying-down and
become the wall the *vellath* is keeping. The chamber will not
receive it. The chamber is an unhealed wound, not a course. The
*thol-ennen* burns off and the strike lands. The brothers step
clear. The *vellath* tries again later.

The kit's `35%-HP-remembers-more-of-itself` line is, in the lore
reading, a **deepening of the chime,** not a phase-change in
appearance. The keeping-stone does not glow brighter; it **rings
under itself.** The audio agent should add the *low* resonance layer
at this beat, and the chamber's wall-glow should slow its pulse,
not quicken. *Remembering more of itself* is a verb the lorekeeper
holds against any agent who, in the course of translating it,
introduces an enrage timer or a frenzy state. There is no enrage. The
*vellath* is not angry. It has reached the part of itself where it
remembers being **set true,** and the remembering is the full weight
of the original *thol-ennen* coming back into one chamber's worth of
stone-air. The chime is correspondingly louder. Damage scales by the
kit's numbers. The lore does not.

### Phase three — the chime, broken (the `shard_storm` register)

When the brothers have reduced Geodelich to the kit's third-phase
threshold, the *shard_storm* tell at 800 ms enters the bank. The
volley of fey-cyan lances is, by lore, the keeping-stone **shedding
its remaining unset crystallizations** in a single coordinated cast.
This is, in the slow stone-tongue, *the* vellath *finishing its long
remembering.* It is not desperation. It is **the last laying-down
escaping at once** — every still-pressed *thol-ennen* the
keeping-stone has been holding for the older ward-line, released in
a single chord. The audio agent's *high* resonance layer enters here
and stays. The chamber-glow brightens **once more** and then begins
to fall.

The brothers may interrupt the *shard_storm.* The kit permits it.
The lore permits it. *Interrupting* the long remembering is, by
canon, **the same courtesy as letting it complete** — both are
*release of the long keeping.* The encounter does not punish either
choice. The Wardens of the Mark
(`lore/factions/wardens_of_the_mark.md`) ledger the closing under
**keeping-vigil,** not **keeping-watch** — a *vellath* that has at
last been allowed to stop trying to keep is *vigil* territory, not
*watch.* The verb-difference is canonical. The Bestiary Designer
need not encode it. The Dialogue agent will.

When Geodelich falls, the chime laps once more, slower, **and then
is laid down.** The chamber-glow holds for two breaths and softens
to the cave's ordinary blue. The under-stream sound returns to the
top of the audio mix. The fey-cyan lattice on the floor is **gone**
— the lore-canonical detail is that the lattice was the *thol-ennen*
itself, and the *thol-ennen* has been released, and the floor is now
only floor. The brothers may stand a moment. The loot drops only
after they have stood long enough that the cave has taken the
chime back. The kit's loot table — *crystal_shard, hp_potion_l,
mp_potion, ring_focus, frost_saber, shadow_dagger, crit_amulet,
guardian_core* — falls in the cave's small ordinary way, not in a
fountain.

The *guardian_core* drop is, in the lore reading, **a single
crystallization that did not release.** It is a fragment of the
*vellath-corr* the keeping-stone was, before the Sundering. The
village's existing item-canon already names it (the Smith and the
Trainer both speak of it in dialogue threads queued in
`WORLD_STATE.md`); the lore-reading here only sets down what the
village's word means against what fell. A **guardian-core** is a
*vellath-corr* fragment held as a trinket. Future item-flavor agents
may quote a single line from this document if needed; the line is:
*it was a keeping-stone, once, and a piece of its keeping is still in
this.* No more.

## The closing — *the chime laid down*

The village's verb for the after of a Geodelich-encounter is **the
chime laid down.** Not *the kill.* Not *the win.* The keeping-stone
has at last completed its long remembering; the *thol-ennen* has been
released; the chime is *kerrithen-corr* — laid down by the
laying-down's own action, not by a mason's hand. The brothers walk
back up through the Crystal Caves, past the under-stream's lip and
past the wall whose crack is still there (the Sundering does not
heal in one encounter; the Crystal Caves remain, by `world.md`, the
**unhealed wound**), and out into the upper chambers, and back to
Briarwood by the way they came.

The Wardens of the Mark ledger the closing under **keeping-vigil.**
Maeve's rope adds a **separate** knot — not the eighty-ninth-line
counter, which is the goblin-clearing line, but a **deeper-line**
knot reserved for events that touch the older ward-canon. The
deeper-line knot has, in living memory, been advanced exactly twice
before this run: once when the well's keeping-stone was re-set after
the second Foxthaw of the present knot-rope, and once when the
mason's leaf in `vellums_spine.md` was first turned out of the
foundation course. The Geodelich-closing is the **third advance** of
the deeper-line. Future canon should preserve the two-prior-only
rule by *not retroactively adding* deeper-line advances elsewhere.

Edda, by canon, **does not say anything** when the brothers come
back. She hands stew. The stew is a callback to the Warlord-clearing
posture; the same posture is correct for the Geodelich-closing,
because both are the village's **wound-finishing** verb in two
different keys (Warlord = goblin-fold's *aen-irren* quieted;
Geodelich = older mason-line's *vellath* released). The stew is the
same stew. Edda is the same Edda.

Mara, by canon, **buys nothing** that the brothers carry back from
the Crystal Caves except the *crystal_shard* and *guardian_core*
drops at honest weight. She does not ask where the *guardian_core*
came from. She has already heard it from Maeve's deeper-line knot
clicking in the Vigil shelf, two evenings before the brothers
return.

Hala, by canon, sits the brothers down for **water** before tea
and asks them, in plain Common, what the cave **sounded like.** The
question is the trainer's territory. The brothers' answer is theirs.
The *thirren-vael* word is not surfaced in dialogue; it is
**surfacing-eligible** for a future codex leaf in the mason-cousin
register, gated to *geodelich_cleared && deeper_knot_advanced.*

Roan, by canon, **tips his head once** when the brothers come
through the south plank, the same gesture as for the
Warlord-clearing. He does not know what they have done in the cave
— Roan's territory is the forest-line, not the under-stream — but
the chime has carried up through the rock to the surface in a way
the horses notice, and Roan reads the horses, and the horses are at
ease when the brothers come through, and Roan tips his head for the
horses' ease. *aen-velin-corr,* on the side of the road being a
little quieter for a season.

Bram, by canon, **lights the lintel candle the brothers passed
under** when they left. He has been keeping the candle ready. He
does not say so. The candle goes back into the lintel-niche when
they have come through and sat. Longnight Vigil's posture, applied
out-of-season for a **deeper-line** advance.

Lyra, by canon, **takes the cave-air the brothers carry on their
cloaks** and ladles it into the canopy-quiet of her herb-rack. The
cave-air smells of cold iron (`stag_courts_courtesy.md`); the
herb-rack will hold the smell for two days, and the herbalists'
neighbours will notice, and no one will say anything aloud. *Lyra
never speaks the goblin-tongue aloud* (a Warlord-canon rule); the
*mason-cousin* equivalent is **Lyra never names the keeping-stone.**
She knows what was in the cave. She holds the knowing in the
herb-rack. The herb-rack is its own *thirre,* small, and patient.

If the players walk past the village's witnesses without noticing,
the silence holds anyway. The silence is not for them.

## Withholdings preserved by this document

The following load-bearing withholdings, set by prior runs, are
**preserved unchanged** by this document. Future agents are reminded
that any of these would be a canon break:

- **The Pale Wyrm's name.** The Wyrm is referred to in this document
  only as *the other one Vellum broke against* (mason-canon phrasing
  from `vellums_spine.md`) and as *the deeper-than-cave dark thread*
  that the cracked outer wall lets up. The Wyrm's name is **not**
  set down. The cousin-rule from `pale_wyrm_beneath.md` holds.
- **The pre-Sundering name of the older ward-line whose keeping-stone
  Geodelich was.** Reserved by this document. The older ward-line
  was *a ward-line.* It had, at the older mason-cousin scribes'
  reading, **other stones** in its course; those stones were taken
  back into the *caer-vellis* during the Sundering. The line's name,
  the names of the masons who set the keeping-stone, and the
  identity of the wall the keeping-stone was meant to keep — **all
  three** are reserved. The keeping-stone is the only fragment of
  the line that the present canon lifts to the page. Future agents
  may not invent the wall, the line, or the masons. The keeping-stone
  stands alone. This withholding joins the **fourth-and-fifth
  load-bearing un-names** of the canon (the Halevant captain was
  the fourth; the older ward-line's wall-name is now the fifth).
- **A divine voice in the encounter.** Forbidden. No god speaks at
  the chime. THEME §7 governs. Brigid does not light the chamber.
  Vellum does not steady the floor. Thiar does not cross the
  under-stream. The Hollow King does not name the *kerrithen-corr.*
  Erris does not lay a coin. The chime is the keeping-stone's, and
  the closing is the keeping-stone's, and the gods are listening
  the way the gods have been listening since the Sundering — which
  is to say, *quietly and from a distance.*
- **A grimdark Geodelich.** Forbidden. No bone, no carrion, no
  scream, no torture, no detailed gore. The *crystallized
  necromancer* line in the kit-comments is the **south-country
  priests' wrong reading** of the chime, and the lore reading
  re-grounds it. Future agents may not write Geodelich as a
  ghoul-king, a lich, a wight-cousin, or a death-knight. The
  keeping-stone is a *thing of stone and laying-down,* not a
  thing of bone and rising-up.
- **An angry Geodelich.** Forbidden. The 35%-HP-line in the kit
  reads *remembers more of itself,* not *enrages.* Future audio,
  VFX, or dialogue agents who substitute *enrage, frenzy, fury, or
  rage* break the line. The chime *deepens.* It does not *roar.*
- **A redeemed Geodelich.** Forbidden — by parallel rule to the
  Warlord's *no noble Warlord* canon. Geodelich is not a tragic
  hero. It is a wound that has been allowed, in the closing, to
  *finish keeping.* The brothers do not learn the wall it was meant
  to keep. They do not bring the keeping-stone home. The chime is
  laid down where it falls.
- **A future-sequel Geodelich.** Forbidden. The kit's
  *act-2 capstone* tier is the canonical placement. There is **no**
  *Geodelich the Returned,* *Geodelich the Sundered* (sequel-named),
  *Greater Geodelich,* or *Crystal Tyrant.* The keeping-stone is
  the keeping-stone. When it is laid down, it stays laid down.
  Future bosses in the Crystal Caves must be **other shapes** — a
  fey-court intruder, a steppe-rider's lost mount, a Wyrm-dream
  half-surfaced — and **not** the same keeping-stone re-stood.
- **The deeper-line knot's full canon.** This document advances the
  deeper-line knot to its third recorded advance. The full canon of
  the deeper-line — what advances it, what it counts, what Maeve's
  predecessor named it, what the rope itself is made of — is
  **reserved** for a future Lorekeeper run on Maeve's keep-rope.
  This artifact only confirms the third advance. Future agents may
  not invent the rope's older canon.
